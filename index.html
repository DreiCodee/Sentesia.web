About Sentensia

✏️ What is Sentensia?

Sentensia is a fun, child-friendly learning app that helps learners build better English sentences. Through short lessons and playful activities, learners discover how words, phrases and clauses fit together to make clear, correct sentences.

🎯 Our goal

To help learners arrange sentence elements, apply appropriate word order, and construct grammatically correct English sentences.
